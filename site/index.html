How it works

Fifteen minutes a day is enough. Home Row starts with F and J, adds two keys per lesson, and watches every press, so practice always aims at the key that slows you down.

Nothing to install, nothing to pay. It's free, needs no sign-up, and runs in your browser. Your progress is saved in this browser.
